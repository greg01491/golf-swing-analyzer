## Rigid Panel Option

For the current rig, use the single-piece [78.4 mm checkerboard artwork](calibration-board-rigid-78.4mm.pdf)
instead of reprinting the tiled paper pattern. The PDF page and board trim are both
**432 x 667 mm**. The 5-by-8-square pattern is **392 x 627.2 mm** and keeps the measured
**78.4 mm squares**; the 0.2 mm difference in overall board height is absorbed by a
0.1 mm difference in the white top/bottom borders. It includes a `FIRST INNER CORNER`
cue, target arrow, golfer-side label, printed square-size label, and 100 mm scale check.
The first rigid-panel PDF used an unembedded standard font and was rejected by
Tradeprint preflight. The current file embeds its Vera TrueType label font; re-upload
this revised PDF rather than the earlier copy.

One UK supplier option is [Tradeprint 3 mm Dibond board printing](https://www.tradeprint.co.uk/rigid-board-printing/dibond-boards).
Its product page lists custom sizes, direct printing on white aluminium composite, and
describes Dibond as rigid and resistant to warping. Select a single-sided 3 mm Dibond
panel with custom width **432 mm** and height **667 mm**. The PDF has the exact same page
and trim size; order it at **100% / actual size**, with scaling or "fit to page" turned
off. This artwork has no bleed because the checker pattern is inset on a white panel.
If the order system requires bleed or offers only auto-fit, contact support before
ordering and ask them to preserve the 78.4 mm squares. Do not choose Correx or cardboard
for this calibration target.

Before using the delivered panel, measure the 100 mm scale check and the checker pattern
across both axes. Confirm the panel lies flat and does not flex; a vendor listing is not
proof that the delivered board meets our 2 px / 5% physical acceptance gates. This is a
supplier recommendation and print-ready artwork only; no order has been placed and no
panel has yet been physically validated.

The PDF has **eight A4 landscape pages**, arranged as two columns by four rows with
10 mm overlap. After trimming, the checkerboard is **400 x 640 mm** and uses **80 mm
squares** in the configured 5-by-8-square pattern (4-by-7 inner corners).

1. Print every page in landscape at **actual size / 100%**. Disable “fit to page” and
   verify the first page's scale bar measures exactly 100 mm.
2. Trim the dashed left edge of tiles in column 2 and the dashed top edge of tiles in
   rows 2 through 4. Tiles later than the first row/column may need both trims.
3. Arrange tiles by their row/column labels, align the overlapping checker pattern, and
   tape the back flat to a rigid board. Measure several printed squares and divide by
   their count to confirm the actual square size.
4. Enter the measured square size in the calibration wizard; this PDF is designed at
   80 mm, but printer scaling must be verified rather than assumed.
5. Capture several distinct, stationary board placements with the complete pattern
   visible in both cameras, holding still for each capture. The existing 80 px per-view
   gate still applies; a larger print does not itself pass calibration validation.

The active camera calibration is not changed by printing this board. The wizard still
requires held-out geometry validation, compatible camera settings, and the independent
ground/timing evidence required by the physical acceptance protocol.
