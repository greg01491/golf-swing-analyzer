# v0.1.15

Installer for testing the metric-confidence and calibration-repair changes on the rig.

## Changes

- Corrected metric geometry, signed directions, circular turn separation and head excursion, with per-metric confidence and honest legacy-result handling.
- Added independent held-out calibration validation, camera-setting compatibility checks, and consistent lens correction during reconstruction.
- Added measured ground/target reference capture with repeated level-board placements, marked-origin selection and separate readiness states.
- Added fingerprinted calibration/reference evidence and explicit historical reconstruction that archives previous outputs instead of silently replacing their provenance.
- Kept uncertain measurements out of pass/fail judgments, coaching and comparable history.

## Important Before Testing

Physical shoulder/hip accuracy is **not yet certified**. Both OpenSpec physical-acceptance gates remain open. Software verification passed 249 backend tests, three frontend tests, build, lint, formatting and strict OpenSpec validation; these checks do not replace measurements on the actual rig.

Use fresh calibration captures at the same camera settings/frame mode used for swings. A legacy file or low fitting error alone does not establish compatible camera geometry. After camera validation, follow the wizard for two measured level-board placements and target/side confirmation. Missing, incompatible or insufficient evidence is reported rather than guessed.

Existing results remain inspectable as legacy-unverified. Saved-3D reanalysis recomputes metrics only and cannot fix a bad reconstruction. Explicit reconstruction requires compatible recorded evidence and archives previous calibration, 3D and metrics. Do not delete original footage while validating the new workflow.

See the repository's `docs/calibration-validation.md` for the independent rig acceptance protocol.
